Members of the planetary armed fighting forces. Soldiers deal with planetary surface action, battles and campaigns. Such individuals may also be mercenaries for hire.

Qualification: END 5+, DM-1 for every previous career, DM-2 if aged 30 or higher

## Assignments
- Support: You are an engineer, cook or in some other role behind the front lines
	- Survival: END 5+
	- Advancement: EDU 7+
- Infantry: You are one of the Poor Bloody Infantry on the ground
	- Survival: STR 6+
	- Advancement: EDU 6+
- Cavalry: You are one of the crew of a gunship or tank
	- Survival: INT 7+
	- Advancement: INT 5+
